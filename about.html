ABOUT

Lost and Found

This website helps people report lost belongings, share items they have found, and connect with others to help return items to their owners.

How it works

  1. Create an account or log in.
  2. Post a lost or found item with its details.
  3. Browse and search reports, then comment to share useful information.
  4. The person who posted an item can update its claim status.
